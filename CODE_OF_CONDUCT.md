# Code of conduct

This applies to every repository in this organisation: the issue
trackers, the pull requests, the commit messages and the code.

## What is expected

Keep it technical. Say what you observed, what you expected, and what
would change if you were right. Disagree about the code as much as you
like; a patch that is wrong is wrong whoever wrote it, and saying so is
the point of review.

## What is not

Personal attacks, insults, and remarks about anyone's person rather than
their work. Harassment of any kind, in public or in private. Publishing
somebody's private information. Deliberately wasting other people's time.

## Reporting

Write to <joneum@FreeBSD.org>. Reports stay between you and the
maintainer; what happens next depends on what happened, and ranges from
a word to a block.

## Scope

A maintainer may edit, delete or close anything that does not belong, and
may block an account from the organisation. No appeal process is promised
here, because promising one for a project this size would be a fiction.
